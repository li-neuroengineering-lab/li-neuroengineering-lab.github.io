Prospective students

The Li Lab continuously welcomes inquiries from prospective PhD students, thesis-based MS students, and undergraduate or TAMS research trainees interested in neuroengineering, computational and experimental neuroscience, electrophysiology, neuroimaging, preclinical epilepsy models, and photobiomodulation.

Prospective trainees are encouraged to email Lin.Li@unt.edu with a CV or résumé and a brief introduction describing their academic background, research interests, and availability.
